Act I · The Long Watch

From Fleet Admiral Gideon Harlan, to the captain of

Captain,

For fourteen years the Shattered Reach has been quiet. That quiet is over. Three merchant ships have vanished on the Kestrel lanes in six days, and the last signal out of Kestrel was not a distress call. It was a warning.

Command calls it piracy. I am not convinced. Corsairs do not fly with Navy transponders, and they do not vanish without wreckage. Someone is arming them, and someone wants this frontier empty.

You will take out of Meridian and answer that warning. She is the only capital ship I can spare, so bring her home. Every plate of hull you lose comes out of the fleet’s requisition, and the frontier cannot afford to lose you.

  1. 01Answer the call at Kestrel and protect the civilian traffic there.
  2. 02Trace the stolen Navy hardware to whoever is supplying it.
  3. 03Report anything you cannot explain. Anything at all.
